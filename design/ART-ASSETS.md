# Hand-drawn booth artwork

The photobooth uses these illustrations from `public/art/`:

- `booth-sketch.png` — the illustrated booth on the landing page.
- `interior-settings.png` — the booth interior and wall behind the setup controls.
- `display-overlay.png` — a transparent close-up bezel over the live camera preview.
- `sketch-button.png`, `sketch-panel.png`, and `sketch-input.png` — reusable hand-drawn control surfaces with transparent exteriors.

The exterior and interior illustrations use pale lavender pencil lines. The camera feed renders underneath the transparent display opening. Buttons and panels keep their illustrated artwork while their text and accessible interactions remain live HTML.

Fonts are served locally from `public/fonts/` under the included font licenses. Camera images are processed in the browser and are not uploaded.
